What the exporter reads

Records already available
in your account.

Availability depends on your device, account, data, and granted permissions. An empty collection does not mean a measurement was taken.

  • Activity & fitnessSteps, distance, exercise, energy estimates, activity summaries, and available VO₂ estimates.
  • Sleep & health measurementsSleep, heart rate, HRV, oxygen saturation, breathing rate, temperature, body measurements, and other available health metrics.
  • Nutrition & hydrationFood and hydration records when present and authorized.
  • Available heart-health recordsECG and irregular-rhythm notifications when the account supports them and permission is granted.
What to expect

A useful archive,
with clear limits.

Exports use the records supplied by the API. They do not provide every raw wearable sensor signal, guarantee complete history, or validate the accuracy of a device. Data can arrive late, and exports can stop if authorization expires or a service is unavailable.

Google Health access is read-only. Exported files remain in Box until manually removed; revisiting recent dates does not automatically delete older records. This is an export tool, not a medical service or an emergency monitor.
