Terms of use

Draft prepared November 5, 2026 · Effective only after operator approval and publication

1. About the pilot

UbiHealth Lab — Personal Health Export is a personal data-export pilot maintained by Jude Welsch. It copies authorized Google Health records into a designated Box folder. The current pilot is limited to the operator’s own accounts; this website does not offer public registration, participant enrollment, or a paid subscription.

2. Authorized accounts and use

Connect only accounts and storage destinations you are authorized to use. Keep credentials private and follow applicable Google, Box, cloud-provider, and institutional requirements. A connection grants only the permissions shown during authorization; publication of this information site does not authorize access to anyone’s account.

3. Data handling

The privacy policy explains data categories, processing, storage, access, retention, revocation, and deletion. The exporter reads Google Health data without modifying its source records. Exported archives can contain sensitive information, so review access before sharing any folder or downloaded file.

4. Availability and accuracy

API coverage, permissions, device support, synchronization delays, service outages, and token expiry can affect exports. A successful job does not prove that every measurement exists or is accurate. Empty or missing data must not be treated as a confirmed physiological result. The pilot has no guaranteed uptime, complete-history coverage, or clinical accuracy.

5. Personal archiving, not medical care

This exporter does not diagnose, treat, provide medical advice, or monitor emergencies. Records and derived device estimates should not be used as a substitute for professional assessment. Any future study involving participants requires a separate scope and the applicable consent and institutional approvals.

6. Stopping or changing the pilot

The operator can pause scheduling and revoke Google or Box access. Revoking access does not delete prior exports. Remove retained files and copies separately as described in the privacy policy. The pilot may be revised or discontinued; changes to data handling require updated disclosures and any applicable consent.

Questions about the pilot or these terms: judewelsch@gmail.com.
